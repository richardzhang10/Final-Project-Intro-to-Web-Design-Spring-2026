01

Rockfleet Financial Services

PRIVATE CAPITAL ADVISORY SPRING ANALYST · SPRING 2026

At Rockfleet, I support the private capital advisory team on investor outreach and due diligence work. The role has sharpened my attention to detail, my comfort with ambiguous instructions, and my ability to take initiative on independent research without constant supervision.

One project I'm currently presenting to a manager is investment due diligence on White Manna Capital Partners, a healthcare-focused long/short equity hedge fund. The work has involved building a thesis around the manager's strategy, analyzing portfolio positioning, and synthesizing findings into a concise recommendation.

02

Wasserman

STRATEGY & CONSULTING INTERN · SUMMER 2025

At Wasserman (Rebranded recently as THE TEAM), I worked on the strategy and consulting team supporting AT&T partnership initiatives. The role required translating brand objectives into clear deliverables for senior stakeholders and learning how to communicate complex ideas to audiences with very different priorities.

The internship taught me how partnership economics actually work in practice and how much of consulting is just clear writing under pressure. I left with a much better sense of how strategy and value gets sold to external clients.

03

Aflac

FINANCE & SALES INTERN · SUMMER 2024

My summer at Aflac was my first real exposure to the finance side of an insurance business. I rotated between sales support and finance, which gave me a useful early view of how the front and back ends of a financial services firm fit together.

It was also the experience that continued to push me toward finance. Seeing how the numbers got built up from the ground level made me want to work closer to the deals themselves.
