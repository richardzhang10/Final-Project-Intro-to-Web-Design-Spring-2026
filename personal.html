Interests

FIVE THINGS THAT FILL THE HOURS

Basketball

I play pickup games whenever I can find one, and I follow the NBA closely enough to have built a player prop prediction tool using statistics and ML last semester. There's a overlap between sports analytics and finance that I find genuinely interesting.

Biking the City

One of the best things about living in New York is how much of it you can see from a bike. Hudson River Park, Central Park, the Brooklyn Bridge, I definitely try to get out as often as the weather allows.

Trying New Food

NYU's location means I'm walking distance from a different cuisine every block. I keep a running list of places on this app called Beli that I want to try and slowly work through it.

Languages

I speak French and Mandarin Chinese alongside English. Both have been useful in unexpected ways, from conversations in foreign countries to just understanding more of what's happening around me.

Two ways I scratch the same itch – my hyper competitiveness.
